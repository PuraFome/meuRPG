# Glossary

Using the same words in conversations, documents and code avoids a lot of confusion. The first column gives the English term with the Portuguese term the app shows in parentheses. The last column gives the name that appears in the tables, the `.proto` files and the Go code.

| Term | Meaning | In the code |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign (campanha) | The ongoing story of one table. Sessions, player characters, maps and documents belong to it. | `campaign` |
| Member (membro) | A user in a campaign, as master or player. The role holds only for that campaign. | `campaign_member`, `role` |
| Pending member (membro pendente) | Someone who accepted an invite that needs approval and is waiting for the master to approve the character they created (RN-15). Not a member yet: sees only the campaign name and their own character. Becomes a player when the master approves; leaves the campaign when the master refuses. | `status = 'pending'`, `awaiting_approval` |
| Master (mestre) | The role of whoever runs the campaign: creates invites and, later, sessions. Whoever creates a campaign becomes its master. A campaign can have more than one master, and a master can hand the campaign over to another (RN-13). | `master` |
| Player (jogador) | The role of whoever plays in the campaign. Joins through an invite. | `player` |
| Display name (nome de exibição) | The name other members see. The person types it in the app; it never comes from Google. | `display_name` |
| Table (mesa) | The set of campaigns of one master. A handle for a player without Google is unique within the table, not per campaign (RN-17). | `home_dm_user_id`, `dm_user_id` |
| Handle | The nickname that identifies a player without Google: the master's nickname together with the player's nickname, unique within the table (RN-17). | `table_handles.handle_norm` |
| Invite (convite) | A link that **adds** a player to the campaign. Only the token hash is stored. It can require approval ("Exigir aprovação do mestre"): whoever accepts it becomes a pending member, creates the character through the invite, and the master approves or refuses (RN-15). | `campaign_invite`, `requires_approval` |
| Session link (link da sessão) | A link that **takes** a member straight to the live session: `/campaigns/<id>/session`, with no secret in it. It gives no access to someone who is not a member, nor to a pending member. | `/campaigns/<id>/session` |
| Game session (sessão de jogo) | One meeting of the table, with a start and an end, numbered from 1 in each campaign. Only one is open at a time, and starting a session locks the sheets. | `game_session`, `session_number` |
| Live session (sessão ao vivo) | The open game session as seen on the session page: the master and the players see changes at once, without reloading, through a stream that stays open only while the page is visible. | `GetLiveSession`, `WatchGameSession` |
| Session notice (aviso de sessão) | The in-app notice that a session started in one of the person's campaigns (RN-06). The app asks the server every 30 seconds while the tab is visible; there is no notification while the app is closed. | `ListOpenGameSessions` |
| Session snapshot (foto da sessão) | Everything the session page shows, read in one go (the session and the HP). The app reads the snapshot after the stream is ready, and again on every reconnection; stream events only update the snapshot. | `GetLiveSession` (snapshot) |
| HP, slots and hit dice (PV, espaços e dados de vida) | The character numbers that change during play and last from one session to the next: current HP, temporary HP (lost first on damage and not part of the maximum), spell slots used per level, pact slots used (warlock) and hit dice used. The maximums come from the sheet; the master corrects the values during the session (RN-02). | `CharacterVitals`, `character_vitals` |
| Session event (evento da sessão) | A change made at the table, stored in a row that never changes, numbered in the order it happened. It is the session history: the master's HP corrections and the combat changes (attacks, damage, actions, undo). | `session_events`, `seq` |
| Idempotency key (chave de idempotência) | The UUID the app generates for a change and sends again if it has to repeat the request. The server recognises the key and does not apply the change twice. | `idempotency_key` |
| Sign-in session (sessão de login) | A user's login in the app. Not to be confused with the game session. | `auth_session` |
| Sign-in intent (intenção de login) | Something the person asked for before signing in and that the server completes right after the login, such as accepting an invite. Kept only on the server, inside the sign-in state. | `intent`, `IntentHandler` |
| RP scene (cena de RP) | A moment outside combat: a point of interest of the scene kind, with the actions the master chose. The master opens it in the session and everyone sees it. | `map_points` (`kind = scene`), `game_sessions.open_scene_point_id`, `OpenScene` |
| On stage (em cena, o palco) | The NPCs the master puts on the open scene for the players to watch enter and leave, as in a visual novel: up to 4 at a time, in the order they came in, with one speaking. The player sees only the name and the portrait (MR-031). Closing or switching the scene empties the stage. | `stage_npcs`, `PutOnStage`, `TakeOffStage`, `SetSpeaker` |
| NPC portrait (retrato do NPC) | An image from the campaign gallery on an NPC's sheet. It appears on the NPC card in combat (for the master only) and in the scene while the NPC is on stage; with no portrait, the app draws the initials (MR-031). | `portrait_image_id` on the sheet |
| Scene action (ação da cena) | A check the master puts on a scene: a skill, an ability check or a saving throw, with an optional name and DC. The player sees their own bonus and sees the DC only when the master turns on "Mostrar a CD aos jogadores". | `scene_actions`, `SceneAction` |
| Attempts (of a scene action) (tentativas) | How many times each player can roll a scene action while it is open: the master sets it per action (1 by default, from 1 to 5, or unlimited) and can give one more attempt to a character. Closing and opening the scene resets the counts (MR-015). | `scene_actions.max_attempts`, `GrantSceneAttempt` |
| Show the DC to players (mostrar a CD aos jogadores) | The per-scene switch with which the master decides whether players see the DC of the actions and whether they passed their own rolls. Off by default; the master always sees the DC (MR-015, RN-20). | `map_points.show_dc` |
| Session summary (resumo da sessão) | What the table sees when the session ends: the duration, the combats and the scenes, the highlights of all combats added together, and "Mais testes passados fora do combate", which counts only rolls from scenes that showed the DC. The master sees every number and the per-player table; the player sees the winners and their own result (MR-032). | `GetSessionSummary` |
| Hooks and notes (ganchos e anotações) | The private text the master keeps on an RP scene: what to say, ideas, hooks. Only the master reads it. | `map_points.hooks` |
| Clue (pista) | A short sentence the master prepares on an RP scene and reveals, during the session, to the players they choose. Whoever receives it reads it in their notes, as "Pista do mestre", and it cannot be hidden again. | `scene_clues`, `scene_clue_reveals` |
| Discovered scene (cena descoberta) | An RP scene the group has already seen: the master revealed the point on the map or opened it in a session. Only a discovered scene works as a label on a note, and it holds for the whole group. | `scene_discoveries` |
| Treasure (tesouro) | A map point with a value in gp (a chest, for example), placed by the master before the session. When the group finds it, the master marks it and says who found it, and "Voltar à cidade" converts the chosen treasures into XP, 1 per gp, in a single award (RN-09). Once found, everyone who sees the map sees it, with the description, the value and who found it. The map stores and marks the treasure, and the XP conversion exists on the server and in the screen (MR-041). | `treasure` (point kind), `treasure_found_at`, `MarkTreasureFound` |
| Return to town (voltar à cidade) | The moment the master converts the treasures the group found into XP: a single gold-mode award, at 1 XP per gp, split equally, rounded down, among the living characters the master picks (all come ticked). Only in a gold campaign; each treasure is converted once, and undoing the award, when it is the last one, returns the treasures to "found, not converted" (MR-041, RN-09). It is a button next to "Dar XP", with the arithmetic written out. | `AwardXP` with `treasure_point_ids`, `ListTreasuresToConvert`, `treasure_converted_award_id` |
| Note (anotação) | The private text a player writes in the campaign (up to 2,000 characters, 300 per campaign), with a discovered scene as a label if they want. Only the author reads it: not even the master. | `player_notes`, `NotesService` |
| Scene roll (rolagem da cena) | The d20 of a scene action plus the character's bonus (in the app or typed, RN-18). The player sees only the total of their own rolls, and "Passou" or "Não passou" only if the master shows the DC; the master sees all of them, under "Rolagens", with "Passou" or "Não passou" when the action has a DC and "Tentativa 1 de 3". Each action rolls up to its attempt limit (1 by default) while the scene is open; with no attempts left, the line says "Sem mais tentativas" and "Rolada". | `SceneRoll`, `RollSceneCheck` |
| Action economy (economia de ação) | What the character can spend in a turn: one action, one bonus action, one reaction and movement (the speed, doubled after Dash). The engine computes what is still available (MR-014). | `Economy`, `TurnOptions` |
| Resource (recurso) | An ability with limited uses, such as Second Wind (1 use per short rest), Ki or Rage. The sheet states the maximum and when it comes back; the session counts the uses spent. | `Resource`, `Derived.Resources` |
| Reason code (código de motivo) | The reason an option is disabled, such as `NO_SLOT` or `ACTION_USED`. The server sends only the code; the app writes the sentence in Portuguese. | `DisabledReason` |
| Encounter (encontro) | A combat on a map, with initiative, rounds and turns. It has three states: setup (`setup`, choosing who fights and rolling initiative), in progress (`active`) and ended (`ended`). A session has at most one that has not ended. | `encounter` |
| Combatant (combatente) | A character inside an encounter; an NPC enters as copies ("Goblin 1", "Goblin 2"), each with its own label. It stores the initiative, the position, the movement, the HP (NPCs only) and whether it is hidden from that combat. | `combatant` |
| Initiative (iniciativa) | The roll that sets the turn order in an encounter. Each combatant rolls its own, including identical NPCs (RN-19). | `initiative` |
| Grid (grade) | The square grid of the map in combat. Each square is 1.5 m, diagonals included (RN-21). The master sets how many squares fit across the map; the rows follow the image proportion. | `grid`, `grid_columns` |
| Sheet (print) (folha de impressão) | A piece of the map printed on one page, at the table's scale (MR-033). Sheets are glued together by the 1 cm overlap each one repeats from its neighbour, and each carries a name: the row letter (top to bottom) and the column number (left to right), such as B2. | `sheet` |
| Pending damage (dano pendente) | The damage of an attack that hit, between the d20 and the end: still to be rolled, rolled and waiting for the master (on a player character), or already applied or discarded. The master applies ("Aplicar 5 de dano") or discards ("Não aplicar"); passing the turn with open damage asks for confirmation (RN-02). | `pending_damages`, `PendingDamage` |
| Combat log (registro do combate) | The list, from the last round to the first, of what happened in the combat: attacks, actions, movements, start and end. The player receives only what they see (nothing from a hidden combatant, the master's dice or an NPC's HP, RN-20); the master receives everything, with "Só o mestre vê" on the lines players do not get. It comes from the session events. | `ListCombatLog`, `CombatLogEntry` |
| Undo (desfazer) | Stepping back one step of the last action (attack, damage, apply, action, spell, reaction, death save, condition or an NPC's HP; not the confirmation of a death), master only: a compensating event restores what was there before, and the history keeps both. | `UndoLastAction`, `action_undone` |
| Prone (derrubado) | The SRD condition `condition:prone` (lying on the ground), which the master sets in "Condições…". It is called "Derrubado", and not "Caído", so it is not confused with a character at 0 HP. | `condition:prone` |
| Down (caído) | A player character at 0 HP who is still making death saves. Everyone who sees them receives the word, never the numbers. It is not the "Derrubado" condition (lying on the ground, `condition:prone`), which the master sets separately. | `COMBATANT_STATE_DOWN` |
| Stable (estável) | A character at 0 HP with three death save successes: stops rolling and stays at 0 HP, unconscious. Everyone sees the word. Becoming stable clears both counts; damage makes them start again with one failure (RN-03). | `COMBATANT_STATE_STABLE` |
| Dying (morrendo) | A character at 0 HP with three failures, waiting for the master to confirm the death. Only the master sees the word; players see "Caído" and the counts (RN-03). | `COMBATANT_STATE_DYING` |
| Master's turn (vez do mestre) | What the player sees when the one acting is a combatant hidden from them: no name, just "Vez do mestre" (RN-10, RN-20). | `master_turn` |
| Enemy state (estado do inimigo) | The word the player sees in place of an NPC's HP: Ileso (unhurt), Ferido (hurt), Muito ferido (half or less) or Derrotado (defeated) (RN-20). | `CombatantState` |
| Initiative tie (empate de iniciativa) | Two combatants with the same total and the same bonus: the master decides who goes first (RN-19). | `tie_unresolved` |
| Death saving throw (teste contra a morte) | The roll of a character at 0 HP, at the start of their turn: 10 or more is a success, less is a failure, a natural 1 is two failures and a natural 20 brings them back with 1 HP. Damage at 0 HP counts one failure (two on a critical hit). On the third failure, they die only when the master confirms (RN-03). | `RollDeathSave`, `death_save_due` |
| Concentration (concentração) | Maintaining a spell that requires it. Casting another concentration spell ends the first; when the concentrating character takes damage, the app reminds the check: DC 10 or half the damage, whichever is higher (RN-22). The app only reminds; the table rolls. A character concentrates on one spell at a time, also on a long casting that has not finished (RN-31). | `concentration_spell`, `concentration_dc` |
| Condition (condição) | An SRD label the master sets on a combatant (poisoned, frightened, prone...). The app shows and reminds, and applies no effect (RN-22), except when a spell that reads HP grants it on casting (Sleep gives Unconscious). | `conditions` |
| HP-reading spell (magia que lê PV) | A spell whose effect depends on the hit points of those in the area, not on damage: Sleep and Colour Spray (a dice total that goes through creatures from lowest to highest HP), Power Word Stun and Power Word Kill (an HP threshold), Spare the Dying (only at 0 HP) and Heal (fixed healing). The server resolves it with the real HP, and the player never sees an enemy's HP (RN-20). | `effects/spells.json`, `hp_pool`, `hp_threshold`, `zero_hp_target`, `flat_heal` |
| HP pool (total (pool) de PV) | The dice Sleep and Colour Spray roll: the total is how many HP of creatures the spell affects. The caster sees their own roll (`5d8 (2, 4, 1, 5, 3) = 15`); the master also sees each creature's HP and what is left. | `SpellCast.pool_roll` |
| Reaction (reação) | The action that fits once per round, off the character's turn or on it, and that returns at the start of the combatant's turn. In the app: Shield when a hit lands, and the opportunity attack. | `reaction_used`, `UseReaction` |
| Reaction prompt (aviso de reação) | The notice "Você foi atingido: usar Escudo Arcano?" that the hit player and the master receive while the hit waits. It says who attacked and with what, if the player sees the attacker; it never carries the hit total or the attacker's AC. | `reaction_prompts` |
| Opportunity attack (ataque de oportunidade) | A melee attack off turn that spends the reaction instead of the action. Whoever leaves the reach of an enemy that can see them, without using Disengage, provokes it: the server makes an **offer** to whoever controls the enemy (the move lands at once and the mover's turn waits for the answer; RN-21). | `as_reaction`, `opportunity_offers` |
| Forced movement (movimento forçado) | A move the GM makes on the combat map for a teleport (Misty Step), a push or a pull: the token goes to the square without provoking an opportunity attack, and closed doors on the way neither open nor stop it. The box "Movimento forçado" turns it on for one drag. | `MoveCombatantRequest.forced` |
| Disengage (desengajar) | The standard action that stops the rest of the turn's movement from provoking opportunity attacks (`standard:disengage`, the `disengaged` mark, cleared at the start of the turn). | `disengaged` |
| Extra Attack (ataque extra) | The feature that lets the Attack action make more than one attack (two at 5th level for fighter, barbarian, monk, paladin and ranger). | `attacks_per_action`, `attacks_left` |
| Character (personagem) | Has a kind: player, enemy, boss, minion or story NPC. A player character who dies is not deleted: it stays in the system, as the base of another character or as a master's NPC in another campaign (RN-03, RN-04). | `character`, `kind` |
| Sheet (ficha) | A character's rules data. Full for player, enemy and boss; basic (HP, AC, speed, initiative and up to three attacks with dice) for minion and story NPC. | `sheet`, `FullSheet`, `BasicSheet` |
| Sheet lock (trava da ficha) | The moment the player stops editing their own sheet: the start of the campaign's first session, or of the next session for a character created later. The character story has its own lock, which the master releases. | `sheet_locked_at`, `story_editing_allowed` |
| Character state (estado do personagem) | Where the character is in the lifecycle: draft, locked, dead or pending approval. The server computes it on every read; an NPC is always a draft. | `CharacterState` |
| Character story (história do personagem) | Personality, appearance, backstory and allies: the text that describes the character, outside the rules. After the sheet lock, the player edits it only when the master allows (story release), until the next session. | `CharacterStory`, `story_editing_allowed` |
| Character revision (revisão do personagem) | A number that goes up with every change to the name, the sheet or the story. If the character changed after the app opened it, saving is refused and the person reloads the sheet. | `revision` |
| Master notes (notas do mestre) | What the master writes about a character, kept separately per campaign. Only the master reads and edits them; they never reach the player (RN-11). | `character_master_notes` |
| Campaign document (documento da campanha) | The text where the master prepares the campaign (MR-018): one per campaign, in Markdown, with gallery images and links to maps and sheets that open in a window. Only the master reads and edits it. It has a revision, like the character: if the document changed after the app opened it, saving is refused. | `campaign_documents`, `CampaignDocument` |
| Rules content (SRD 5.1) (conteúdo de regras) | The set of races, classes, spells and rules the app knows. It comes from the SRD 5.1 (CC-BY-4.0) and has a version, such as `srd51@a8abc93b235c+fx.1`, which each sheet stores (the screen does not show it: it is an internal detail). | `content_version` |
| Ability (habilidade) | One of the character's six abilities: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma. It covers the score ("valor de habilidade"), the modifier ("modificador de habilidade") and the check ("teste de habilidade"). It is never a skill ("perícia" stays skill) nor a class feature. The increase is the official feature "Incremento no Valor de Habilidade" (Ability Score Improvement). | `ability`, `ability_scores` |
| Spell level (nível da magia) | The level of a spell or a spell slot, from 1 to 9 ("magia de 1º nível", "espaço de magia de 2º nível"); a cantrip is level 0 and stays "Truque". Where it could be confused with the character level, the text says "nível da magia" and "nível do personagem". "Nível" alone is left only for geometry (the circle range of movement) and subclass names (the druid's "Círculo da Terra"). | `spell_level`, `level` |
| Saving throw (teste de resistência) | The roll with which a creature avoids or reduces an effect ("teste de resistência de Destreza", "CD do teste de resistência"). | `saving_throw`, `save` |
| Official names (nomes oficiais) | The Portuguese names of spells, classes, features, conditions, items and creatures follow the terminology of the Brazilian editions of the Player's Handbook and the Dungeon Master's Guide. Only names and short terms, never the books' text. A product decision wins when there is one ("Derrubado", not "Caído", for `condition:prone`), and so does the SRD 5.1 name when the official one carries a WotC proper name the SRD dropped ("Esfera Congelante", not "... de Otiluke"; "Disco Flutuante", not "... de Tenser"). The general terms follow the book too: "habilidade", spell "nível" and "teste de resistência"; see the entries "Ability", "Spell level" and "Saving throw". | `names_pt.json` |
| Content key (chave de conteúdo) | The stable identifier of a rules item, such as `class:wizard` or `spell:fire-bolt`. The sheet stores keys, never names. | `key` |
| Sheet choices (build) (escolhas da ficha) | What the player chose: base abilities, race, classes, background, skills, spells and equipment. The resulting numbers are never stored. | `Build`, `FullSheet` |
| Derived values (valores calculados) | The sheet numbers the server computes on every read, from the choices and the rules content. The browser never computes a rule. | `DerivedSheet`, `rules.Derive` |
| Effect (efeito) | The rule of a feature or trait written as data: a bonus, a proficiency, a sense, a hint. | `effect` |
| Formula (fórmula) | The short calculation of an effect, such as `8 + prof() + mod("int")` for the spell save DC. It runs in the Expr library, with a closed list of what is allowed. | `formula` |
| Hint (dica) | A bonus or advantage that depends on the situation, such as Gnome Cunning. The sheet shows it; the master decides when it applies. | `hint` |
| Sheet issue (pendência da ficha) | A choice outside the rules or an unknown key. The sheet opens all the same and shows the warning: the app helps, it does not judge. | `issue` |
| Custom background (antecedente personalizado) | The free-text background (the "Outro" of the character editor), by the SRD 5.1 rule "Customizing a Background": a name, two skills, two tools or languages in total (in any mix), one feature (name and the player's text) and the equipment (text). The sheet computes with it; what is missing is a warning, never an error. | `custom_background`, `CustomBackground`, `proficiency_keys` |
| Effects revision (revisão de efeitos) | The number that goes up with every change to the effects we write. A content version never changes in place. | `fx.<n>` |
| Character copy (cópia de personagem) | A new character made from another, to play in another campaign. Once copied, each goes its own way. | `copied_from_id` |
| Gallery (galeria) | A campaign's images, which the master uploads to use in maps and in the campaign document (MR-019). Only the master sees the gallery; a player sees an image only while it is shown to them, on a map they see, shown in the session or left with them. Each image is stored without metadata (EXIF, GPS) and has a thumbnail. | `gallery_images`, `GalleryService` |
| Generated image (Imagem gerada) | A gallery image the GM asked an AI model for (MR-039, RN-28), in three ways: scene art (or the adjustment of a generated image), the isometric view of a map and the textured map, which matches the grid and can become the map's own image. The isometric view and the scene art made from a map show only what the players see now. It enters the gallery hidden from the players, like any image, until the GM shows it, and carries the provider's invisible watermark (SynthID). An adjustment points to the image it started from (the adjustment chain). | `gallery_images.generated`, `parent_image_id`, `ImageGenerationService`, `ImageGenService` |
| Monthly slot (vaga do mês) | One of the images a campaign can generate per month (RN-28). Reserved when requested; returned if nothing is generated or if the master cancels before the request leaves the server; spent if the image comes back, or if the master cancels after the request left. | `image_requests`, `IMAGE_MONTHLY_LIMIT` |
| Players' view (vista dos jogadores) | What the players' characters on a map see now: the union of each one's sight (the fog computation, without what they remember), the creatures they see, and the unrevealed secret door as a wall. It is where the scene art and the isometric view made from a map start (RN-28); on a map without fog, it is the whole map minus what is hidden. It becomes a small drawing sent to the AI service as a reference and is never shown to a player. | — |
| Map (mapa) | A gallery image with points of interest and tokens on top (MR-008). Born hidden; a player sees it only after the master reveals it, or while it is the session's current map. | `maps`, `Map`, `MapService` |
| Point of interest (ponto de interesse) | A place on the map that opens a battle, a submap or an RP scene. It has a name, a description for the players and the position on the image. Born hidden. | `map_points`, `MapPoint` |
| Submap (submapa) | A map opened from a point of interest of another map, such as the tower inside the region. The submap point leads to it; the player only enters if they also see the submap. | `MAP_POINT_KIND_SUBMAP`, `target_map_id`, `parent_maps` |
| Token | The marker of a character on a map: a player's is born visible, an NPC's is born hidden, and the master reveals it whenever they want. The master places, moves, hides and removes; the player sees the visible token move at once. | `map_tokens`, `MapToken` |
| Reveal, hidden (revelar, escondido) | Showing a map, a point or a token to the players, or hiding it again (RN-10). What is hidden never leaves the server for the player: not even the ID. On the master's screen, hidden is dashed, with a crossed-out eye and the word "Escondido". | `revealed_at`, `hidden`, `SetMapRevealed` |
| Current map (mapa atual) | The map the master shows to everyone in the open session. Choosing the current map reveals it; a new session starts with no current map. | `game_sessions.current_map_id`, `SetCurrentMap` |
| Shown image (Imagem mostrada) | A gallery image the GM shows the players during the session, such as a portrait or a handout (MR-028). One at a time, next to the current map; it reveals nothing else. When the GM stops showing it, the image disappears from the players' screens and they lose access to it, unless the GM left it with them (see Left image). | `game_sessions.shown_image_id`, `SetShownImage` |
| Left image (Imagem deixada) | A shown image the GM left with the players ("Deixar com os jogadores", MR-028). It stays on their session page, under "Imagens que o mestre deixou", until the GM takes it back ("Tirar"), even after the session ends. It belongs to the campaign, not to the session. | `campaign_left_images`, `ListLeftImages`, `TakeBackLeftImage` |
| Position in base points (Posição em pontos-base) | Where a point or a token sits on the image: 0 to 10000 across the width and the height (5000 is the middle). It does not depend on the image size in pixels. | `x_bp`, `y_bp` |
| Dungeon (Masmorra) | A map drawn with walls (including false ones), floor, water, doors, traps and chests (normal or mimics). | `dungeon` |
| Dungeon generator (Gerador de masmorras) | The GM tool that generates a dungeon (rooms, corridors, doors, stairs, with size and style options) as a map the GM can edit (MR-010). Built by clean-room design. | — |
| Hooks and clues (Ganchos e pistas) | The GM's private notes for each RP scene: hooks, clues and what to say. A clue can be revealed to the players (MR-029). | — |
| Player notes (Anotações do jogador) | Each player's private notepad, always at hand. A note can carry the label of a scene the player has already discovered (MR-030). | — |
| Combat highlights (Destaques do combate) | The end-of-combat screen for the table: who dealt the most damage, healed the most, took the most damage ("tank"), landed the most killing blows and hit the most critical hits, counting only HP that really changed (MR-032). | CP, SP, EP, GP and PP (PC, PP, PE, PO e PL) | The treasure coins: copper piece (PC), **silver** piece (PP, "peça de prata": 10 PP = 1 PO), electrum (2 PE = 1 PO), gold (PO) and platinum (1 PL = 10 PO). One hundred PC are worth 1 PO. The value of a pile of coins is rounded down, in whole PO. Only coins, gems and works of art become the gold of a treasure point; magic items never become XP (MR-044, RN-09). | `TreasureCoinKind` |
| SRD 5.2.1 values (2024 rules) (Valores do SRD 5.2.1 (regras de 2024)) | The label of magic item prices (common 100 PO, uncommon 400, rare 4,000, very rare 40,000, legendary 200,000; the artifact has no price): they come from the "Magic Item Rarities and Values" table of SRD 5.2.1, even though the items are from SRD 5.1. A consumable is worth half; the Spell Scroll is worth the full value (an engineering decision). | `GetMagicItemResponse.value_label` |
| Generated dungeon (Masmorra gerada) | A map made by the dungeon generator from the size, the options and a seed (the same request gives the same dungeon), with the walls and doors already in the layers (MR-010). "Redesenhar" redraws the image, at the same size, from the current walls and doors, without erasing the layers. The map is born hidden, with fog on and the base light bright, and the list of rooms, the seed and the options are the GM's only; the stairs are born revealed, and the walls layer covers all the rock. The screens: the "Gerar masmorra" page, the list of rooms and "Redesenhar". | `generated_dungeons`, `rules/dungeon`, `DungeonService` |
| Area spell (Magia de área) | A spell that affects whoever is in a shape (cone, cube, cylinder, line or sphere, with the size in metres). In combat, the caster chooses the creatures it affects; the app does not draw the area on the map. A table spell can have an area. | `max_targets = 0` |
| Self and Touch range (Alcance Pessoal e Toque) | The range of a spell that only reaches the caster, or an area that comes out of them ("Pessoal", the SRD's `Self`), and that of one that requires touch ("Toque", 1.5 m in combat). A table spell can have both, besides a distance; Self only applies with "only the caster" or with an area. | `RangeSelf`, `RangeTouch` |
| Stage (Etapa) | A phase of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md). We say "etapa" to avoid confusion with the XP milestone. | — |
| Attunement (Sintonização) | A character's bond with a magic item that requires it ("requires attunement" in the SRD): the item only works for whoever attuned to it. Some items limit who can ("por um paladino", "por um conjurador"). An attunement that applies to a single property is in the item's text. | `Attunement`, `AttunementBy` |
| Rarity (Raridade) | How hard a magic item is to find: common, uncommon, rare, very rare, legendary or artifact. "Varia" marks the family whose variants have different rarities (the Potion of Healing). | `Rarity`, `varies` |
| Item family, variant (Família de itens, variante) | A magic item that the SRD lists in several versions: the family (Armor +1, +2 or +3; Ring of Resistance) and the variants (Armor +1; Ring of Fire Resistance), each with its own key and rarity. In the treasure draw, a family counts as one unit per rarity, not one per variant. | `Variants`, `VariantOf`, `MagicItemUnit` |
| Ritual (ritual) | A spell with the ritual tag cast without a slot, taking its casting time plus 10 minutes. Only a class with Ritual Casting can: the bard (known), the cleric and the druid (prepared), the wizard (spellbook). The screen offers it as "Como ritual" (RN-31). | `ritual`, `as_ritual` |
| Long casting (conjuração longa, "Concluir conjuração") | A cast of a spell that takes minutes or hours, outside a combat. It starts in two steps: the caster concentrates and nothing is spent; the master taps "Concluir conjuração" when the game time has passed, and then the slot is spent and the spell takes effect. A broken concentration fails it with no slot spent (RN-31). | `OUTSIDE_CAST_STATUS_CASTING`, `ConfirmCastTimePassed` |
| Active spell (magia ativa) | A spell that took effect and lasts. It ends by the master or the caster ("Encerrar"), by the caster's concentration, or by a rest that covers its whole duration. The screen lists them in "Magias ativas" (RN-31). | `spell_casts`, `EndActiveSpell` |
