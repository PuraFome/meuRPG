# Delivery history by Etapa

When each Etapa of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md) reached `main`, which PRs carried it, which migrations it added, and how the slices ("fatia 10.x" in the original notes) map to what they delivered. Nothing here is current behaviour: the docs describe what the system does ([architecture](../architecture.md), [data model](../data.md), [operations](../operations.md), [design](../design.md), [product](../product/rules.md)). Who decided what, and when, is in [decisions.md](decisions.md).

Every PR number is a PR of `PuraFome/meuRPG`. Dates are dd/mm/2026. Each Etapa delivered, for each part, the server, the screens (designed and approved before the code) and the Go and Playwright tests of every acceptance criterion.

### Etapa 1: Foundation

Monorepo, Go server, Protobuf contracts, CI and local environment. On `main` since 30/09/2026 (PRs #3 and #4); the first deploy was to come afterwards. No stories.

### Etapa 2: Test base and web

Playwright against the local stack, with a local OIDC provider in place of Google; the skeleton of the new Angular app in `web/`; Go integration tests in CI. On `main` since 30/09/2026 (PRs #5 and #7): the `web/` skeleton, the devidp (development OIDC provider), the Playwright tests in `e2e/` (`make e2e` and the `e2e` workflow) and the CockroachDB integration tests in CI (the `go-db` job). No stories.

### Etapa 3: Sign-in and campaigns (MR-001, MR-002, MR-003)

Master sign-in by OIDC with sessions of up to 30 days, create a campaign, generate and revoke invites, join by invite (signed in, or signing in on the way), with authorization by campaign role (ADR-0011) and a display name. On `main` since 30/09/2026 (PRs #6 and #8 to #10): backend, screens and Playwright tests of the three stories. The character created through the invite (MR-003) came with Etapa 4.

### Etapa 4: Characters (MR-004, MR-005, MR-006, MR-024)

PDF-format sheet, NPCs, sheet locked at the first session, the character created through the invite (the half of MR-003 that was missing) and the master's approval of that character (MR-024, in the MVP since 29/09/2026). ### MR-001: Create a campaign

### MR-003: Join by invite

- Backend for joining the campaign done on 29/09/2026: with a login, the invite becomes a membership as a player; accepting again changes nothing; two players competing for the last use do not both get in. Tests: `TestMR003_ValidInviteMakesTheUserAPlayerTheMasterSees` (first criterion, without the character part) and `TestMR003_ExpiredInviteGivesAClearErrorAndCreatesNothing`.
- Backend for "open the link and log in" done on 29/09/2026: someone who is not logged in signs in and accepts the invite in a single step (`POST /auth/login` with the `campaign_invite` intent), without storing anything in the browser, and lands on `/campaigns/<id>` or on `/invite/error?reason=<code>`. Go tests, with a fake provider and CockroachDB: `TestSignInWithAnInviteJoinsTheCampaign`, `TestSignInWithAnUnusableInvite` and `TestSignInWithAnInviteAsAMember`.
- Screen done on 29/09/2026: `/invite` (`web/src/app/pages/invite/invite-accept.ts`), which reads the token from the URL fragment, erases it from the URL right away (`history.replaceState`) and accepts the invite (logged in) or offers "Entrar para aceitar o convite" (logged out). Playwright test, with a second user already logged in: `jogador já logado abre o link do convite, entra na campanha e o mestre o vê nos membros` (a logged-in player opens the invite link, joins the campaign and the master sees them among the members) (`@MR-003`, `e2e/tests/invite.spec.ts`). The path of someone who opens the link logged out depends on the sign-in-with-invite contract (`intent=campaign_invite`), not yet integrated in this branch: the test `visitante sem sessão entra pelo convite, faz login e é adicionado à campanha automaticamente` (a visitor without a session opens the invite, signs in and is added to the campaign automatically) is written as `test.fixme` in the same file, to be switched on when the backend arrives. And the no-session path, through the login: `visitante sem sessão entra pelo convite, faz login e é adicionado à campanha automaticamente` (`@MR-003`), which also checks that the token does not appear in any URL requested by the browser.
- Character backend done on 29/09/2026 (Etapa 4): the player creates their own character, of the player type, with `CharacterService.CreateCharacter`, and the master sees it in the campaign list, with the player's display name, class and race (`ListCharacters`). The character is born as a draft. Test: `TestMR003_PlayerCreatesTheirCharacterAndTheMasterSeesIt`. The screen for creating the character (the step-by-step editor) came in the Etapa 4 screens PR, with the Playwright test "o jogador entra pelo convite, cria o personagem e o mestre o vê na campanha" (the player joins by invite, creates the character and the master sees it in the campaign).

### MR-004: Sheet in the PDF format

- Backend done on 29/09/2026 (Etapa 4): `CharacterService.GetCharacter` returns the sheet as the player filled it in and, together with it, the values calculated by the server (`DerivedSheet`): abilities and modifiers, saving throws, skills, passives, AC, HP, speed, senses, spell save DC and spell attack, slots, spells, attacks, features and the sheet's pending items. Test: `TestMR004_SheetComesWithServerCalculatedValues`, with Pensantus (INT 18, +4; DC 14; spell attack +6; AC 13; HP 23). The sheet screen came in the Etapa 4 screens PR, with the Playwright test "o jogador abre a ficha no celular e vê as seções da ficha oficial com os valores calculados pelo servidor" (the player opens the sheet on the phone and sees the official sheet's sections with the values calculated by the server).
- The sheet editor (02/10/2026) offers "Nenhuma" for the subclass, says at which level the class chooses it ("O Bárbaro escolhe a subclasse no nível 3."), and changes the subclass when the class changes. In the "Magias" step, it lists only the spells up to the highest level of the current level, ordered by level and then by name; a spell already chosen above the limit stays in the list, marked "acima do nível", so it can be unchecked, and classes that start casting later (Paladin, Ranger) show "O Paladino conjura magias a partir do nível 2." at level 1. "Truques" only appears for a class that has cantrips on its list (the Paladin and the Ranger do not), or when the sheet already has a cantrip, so it can be unchecked. The highest level per level comes from the server (`ClassSpellcasting.max_spell_level_by_level`, in `ContentService.ListContent`). Tests: `TestMaxSpellLevelFromSlots`, `TestCatalogMaxSpellLevelByLevel` (`rules`), `TestCatalogToProtoMaxSpellLevel` (`characters`), those in `character-editor.spec.ts` ("the subclass" and "the spell lists by level") and the Playwright `o editor da ficha mostra só as magias do nível e deixa tirar a subclasse` (the sheet editor shows only the spells of the level and lets you remove the subclass) (`@MR-004`, `e2e/tests/character-editor.spec.ts`).
- The creation rolls and the spells' "?" (02/10/2026, Etapa 6, at Samuel's request). In the "Habilidades" step, "Como definir os valores" has three cards: "Digitar" (the usual six fields), "Rolar 4d6" and "Conjunto padrão". "Rolar 4d6" rolls 4d6 six times in the browser (`crypto.getRandomValues`, unbiased), strikes out the lowest die of each, lists the results from highest to lowest and has "Rolar de novo", which swaps all six and undoes the placement. The player assigns each result to an ability: from tablet size up, each ability becomes a selector (choosing a result that is on another ability swaps the two); on the phone, you tap the result ("Escolhido") and then the ability, with the words "Livre", "Escolhido" and "em Força". "Conjunto padrão" does the same with 15, 14, 13, 12, 10 and 8, without dice. While any result is left without an ability, the editor does not save and says "coloque cada resultado numa habilidade" (it never turns into six 10s without anyone seeing). Nothing is stored: the sheet only receives the number placed (question 40). In "Pontos de vida", "Rolado" shows one line per level from the 2nd ("Nível 2 (1d12)"), "Rolar", "Rolar os níveis que faltam" (only the empty ones; you can type what came up on the table die), the formula "1d12 (8) + 3 (Constituição) = 11 PV" with the final Constitution (value, race, subrace and manual bonus) and a box with the total so far and the final range; it is a preview, the server recalculates the maximum on save (`rules.Derive`). Beside each spell, a 44 px "?" opens the description (dialog on desktop, bottom sheet on the phone): name, spell level and school, casting time, range (in meters: 5 ft = 1.5 m), components and duration in Portuguese, the Ritual and Concentração tags and the SRD text in English, marked `lang="en"` ("Texto do SRD 5.1 (em inglês)", plus "Em níveis superiores" when there is one), coming from `ContentService.GetSpellDetails`, fetched on the spot and kept only while the page is open. What the formatter cannot translate appears as the raw SRD text, with the note "(texto do SRD)". Tests: `dice.spec.ts`, `hit-points-preview.spec.ts`, `spell-details-format.spec.ts`, `ability-scores.spec.ts`, `hit-points-rolls.spec.ts`, `spell-details.spec.ts` and those of "rolls and the spell \"?\"" in `character-editor.spec.ts`; Playwright `@MR-004` in `e2e/tests/character-editor.spec.ts` ("rolar 4d6 dá seis resultados…", "o conjunto padrão pode ser colocado e trocado…", "os pontos de vida rolados preenchem todos os níveis que faltam", "o \"?\" ao lado da magia abre a descrição…") and `@a11y` in `a11y.spec.ts` ("as rolagens e a descrição da magia passam no axe…").

**`migration_lock` is not a migration.** It is the lock of `migrate up` and `migrate down` (one row, `id = 1`, with `holder` and `expires_at`), created by the lock's own code (`backend/migrations/lock.go`) before goose, because the lock has to exist before the first migration. A run that crashed leaves the row, and the next one takes it when the validity passes. The times are the database clock's (`now()`), not that of whoever runs it. See [Operations](../operations.md).

### Tables the live table needed, by Etapa (the plan as written during the build)


New tables for the live table:

- `game_sessions`: start and end of each session. Starting a session fills `sheet_locked_at` on the sheets of the players that are still drafts, in the same transaction. It already exists (Etapa 4, see implemented schema).
- `character_vitals`: current HP, temporary HP, spell slots used, hit dice used and spent resource uses of each player character, which last from one session to the next (RN-02). It already exists (Etapa 5, see implemented schema).
- `encounters` and `combatants`: round, turn, initiative, current HP, spell slots used and grid position of each combat.
- `session_events`: each action of the session (damage, healing, spell, XP) becomes a row that is never altered. It is the table's history; the stream sends the change it records. It already exists (Etapa 5), with the master's correction to HP, spell slots and hit dice, and, in Etapa 6, the combat changes: the attack, the damage, the actions, the spells, the reactions, the death saving throws, the conditions, the undo. The combat log is read from it (see implemented schema).
- `opportunity_offers`: the opportunity attack that a move offers to whoever it left behind, until someone answers (MR-034, RN-21, Etapa 9, slice 9.6b).
- `pending_damages`: the damage of an attack or a spell that hit, and the healing, from the d20 until the master applies or discards it (MR-012, MR-014). It already exists (Etapa 6). It also stores a trap's damage in a combat (MR-035, Etapa 9, slice 9.8: no attacker, with `trap_point_id`).
- `trap_damages`: a trap's damage on a player character **outside a combat**, waiting for the master (MR-035, RN-02, Etapa 9, slice 9.8).
- `puzzles`, `puzzle_runs`, `puzzle_moves` and `puzzle_hint_tries`: the campaign's puzzles (master only), the live state of each in a session, one row per move, with the anti-repeat key, and one per attempt to earn a hint by a skill check (MR-038, RN-27, Etapa 10, slices 10.7a and 10.7b). They already exist (see implemented schema).
- `battle_encounters`: the encounter the master stores on a map's battle point (the creatures and quantities, the base names, how HP is chosen and whether the monsters start hidden), one row per point (MR-043, RN-29, Etapa 10, slice 10.9c). Master only (RN-10). It already exists (see implemented schema).
- `xp_awards` and `xp_award_shares`: who gave XP or recorded a milestone, how much, when and why, and what each character received (MR-016). The XP mode is in `campaigns.xp_mode`. They already exist (Etapa 7, see implemented schema).
- `xp_award_treasures`: the treasures a "Voltar à cidade" award converted (MR-041, Etapa 9, slice 9.11).
- `planned_milestones`: the milestones the master writes ahead of time in a milestone campaign (MR-016, Etapa 8, slice 8.10); `xp_awards.milestone_id` links each milestone award to the planned milestone.
- `character_level_ups` and `character_level_up_rolls`: the record of each level-up the player makes on the locked sheet, which the master reads as "O que mudou", and the hit die the server rolled for the next level, kept until the level-up uses it (MR-040). They already exist (Etapa 8, see implemented schema).
- `campaign_table_rules` and `character_ability_rolls`: a campaign's table rules (the level-up HP, the ways to make ability scores, the critical hit, the death saving throws, combat and the fog of new maps, the house rules) and the 4d6 the server rolled for a player's next sheet (MR-025, RN-24). They already exist (Etapa 10, slice 10.4a, see implemented schema).
- `stage_npcs`: the NPCs "on stage" in a session's open scene, in the order they entered, at most 4, with who is speaking (MR-031). It already exists (Etapa 8, see implemented schema).
- `scene_actions`: the actions the master put in an RP scene (MR-015). The scene has no table of its own: it is a map point of type `scene` (`map_points`), and the scene open in the session is the column `game_sessions.open_scene_point_id`. They already exist (Etapa 7, see implemented schema).
- `scene_clues` and `scene_clue_reveals`: the clues the master prepares in a scene and to whom they were revealed, with the copy of the text the player received (MR-029). They already exist (Etapa 8, see implemented schema). The "Ganchos e anotações" text is the column `map_points.hooks`.
- `scene_discoveries`: the scenes the group has already discovered, the only ones a player can use as a tag on a note (MR-030). It already exists (Etapa 8).
- `player_notes`: each player's private notes (MR-030): only the author reads them. It already exists (Etapa 8).

Points of interest live in a table of their own, `map_points`, and tokens in `map_tokens` (done in Etapa 5), each with its own revealed-or-hidden state. The server filters the hidden ones before answering (RN-10). The in-app notification needs no table in the MVP: a `game_session` with no `ended_at` already is the "session in progress" notice.

**Character state.** Decided in Etapa 4: a `status` column (`active`, `dead` or `pending`) next to `sheet_locked_at`. The dead (RN-03) change state, never row. The pending-approval character (RN-15, MR-024) is the character of whoever joined through an invite with approval: it becomes `active` when the master approves, and is deleted when the master refuses, because it never became part of the campaign. The details are in implemented schema.

## Operations and design build notes

### Operations: build notes

How the operations page grew; what exists now is in [operations.md](../operations.md).

- **Hosting and costs.** The Cloud Run plan (São Paulo, 1 vCPU, 512 MiB, `min-instances` 0, `max-instances` 1) and the cost estimates were written before any deploy; the CockroachDB plan was confirmed on 29/09/2026 (see [decisions](decisions.md)). The Cloud Storage price was checked on 30/09/2026 when the gallery (Etapa 5) was designed; the gallery quota (300 images, 500 MiB) was a proposal at that point.
- **Live-session stream (Etapa 5).** The stream rules (heartbeat 25 s, recheck 60 s, 30 minutes maximum life, 8 streams per user per campaign) arrived with `PlayService.WatchGameSession` (ADR-0005).
- **Fog tiles (Etapa 9, slice 9.5).** The tile budget and the memory sum were measured with `TestTileMemory` and `TestTileRequestTiming`; the tile and memory figures are from 04/10/2026 (they live in CONTRIBUTING). The upload-slot figure was corrected in that change: it had been recorded as 230 MiB, because the thumbnail and the reference used a working area the size of the image (77 MB and 163 MB); they now scale in strips of 32 rows (about 8 MB), and the measured upload is 178 MiB.
- **Table content cache (Etapa 10, slice 10.1c).** Measured with `TestLiveContentMemory` and `BenchmarkWith` on 06/10/2026 (Apple M1 Pro).
- **Abuse limits (RN-30, 07/10/2026).** `MAX_CAMPAIGNS_PER_USER`, `CAMPAIGN_CREATORS`, `IMAGE_DAILY_LIMIT` and `RATE_LIMIT_MULTIPLIER` arrived with RN-30.
- **Generated images (Etapa 10, slices 10.8a and 10.8b).** The Gemini price page was checked on 06/10/2026 (US$ 0.067 per 1K image on `gemini-3.1-flash-image`). The memory of a call before the streaming change was up to 14 images of 10 MiB in base64 (about 512 MiB live, 1.36 GiB allocated). The drawing of the largest map (200 × 400) and the `CropFit` worst case (151 MiB) were measured on 06/10/2026 in slice 10.8b.
- **ELK.** The local ELK was written without Docker available, which is why it is marked "not verified live".
