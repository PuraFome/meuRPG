# Columns added to combatants after combatant_columns.txt was frozen, one per line: FieldName # reason.
# FieldName is the field of the Combatant model (backend/internal/play/playdb/models.go), sorted.
# The reason (at least 20 characters) says why the column is not about one feature.
# A feature that must remember a use does not get a column: it keeps an entry in feature_state
# (docs/architecture/play/feature-state.md).
MountKind # a willing mount's setting by the master, read by the view and the rules, not a use of a feature
MountMoveUsed # the once-per-move mark of mounting, cleared with the movement counters at the start of the turn
MountPriorInit # what a controlled mount had before it took its rider's initiative, put back on dismount
MountedOnID # the link rider to mount: a foreign key with a unique index, which feature_state cannot hold
